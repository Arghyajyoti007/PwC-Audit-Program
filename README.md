## 📌 Overview
This repository documents my completion of the **PwC US Audit Virtual Job Simulation** via [Forage](https://www.theforage.com/simulations/pwC-us/audit-w3fr). This practical program provided hands-on experience simulating real-world external audit engagements, internal control evaluations, process walkthroughs, and client communications for the client (**ABC Company**).

---

## 🛠️ Simulation Curriculum & Key Tasks

The simulation covered the core lifecycle of an external audit engagement, focusing on risk assessment, internal controls testing, and formal documentation:

### 1. Task 1: Detailed Questions & Internal Correspondence
* **Objective:** Understand client processes and internal controls for ABC Company's purchasing cycle.
* **Key Actions:** 
  * Developed a detailed list of follow-up questions required to thoroughly comprehend the client’s purchasing procedures.
  * Drafted a professional email addressed to the Audit Senior outlining inquiries and missing information needed for audit planning.

### 2. Task 2: Control Testing & Operational Effectiveness
* **Objective:** Evaluate the operational effectiveness of ABC Company's existing internal controls.
* **Key Actions:**
  * Learned the fundamentals of control testing methodologies.
  * Executed control tests on sample transactions within the purchasing process and formally documented testing results and findings.

### 3. Task 3: Client Walkthrough & Information Gathering
* **Objective:** Participate in a simulated process walkthrough and identify follow-up items.
* **Key Actions:**
  * Reviewed walkthrough data to track material information flows.
  * Highlighted key audit findings, procedural gaps, and necessary action items for effective evidence gathering.

### 4. Task 4: Process Flowcharts & Risk Control Matrix (RCM)
* **Objective:** Synthesize audit findings into structured governance deliverables.
* **Key Actions:**
  * Constructed a comprehensive process flowchart mapping out ABC Company's transaction flows.
  * Developed a Risk Control Matrix (RCM) mapping identified risks to corresponding internal controls.
  * Documented control deficiencies and gaps for executive reporting.

---

## 📈 Key Skills & Competencies Gained
* **Internal Controls Assessment:** Evaluating the design and operational effectiveness of financial controls.
* **Audit Documentation:** Preparing professional working papers, process flowcharts, and Risk Control Matrices (RCM).
* **Client Communication:** Drafting clear, concise professional correspondence and inquiries for audit seniors and clients.
* **Risk Identification:** Pinpointing operational deficiencies and control gaps within transaction cycles.
