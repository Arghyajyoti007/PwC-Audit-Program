# ABC Company Purchasing Policy
*Reviewed and approved for use as of July 1, 2024*

## Overview
This Purchasing Policy outlines the process and controls surrounding an employee’s purchase (procurement) of goods and services on behalf of the ABC Company, when such materials are necessary for fulfilment of an employee’s assigned role.

## Purchasing Process
When an employee would like to make an external purchase from a vendor, the employee first enters the purchase request into the Purchase Request Portal. Here, the employee will enter the type of purchase they’d like to make (for example, “Capital Assets- Computers”) and will populate the desired vendor and anticipated purchase price.

Once the required fields within the Purchase Request Portal are populated, the requestor will click “submit” within the portal. This triggers the request to be automatically routed (via an Organizational Chart embedded within the Portal) to the employee’s direct supervisor. The supervisor receives an automated email that they’ve been assigned a new purchase request for their review/approval.

__Per internal policy, any purchases made by managers or above must be pre-approved if the requested purchase totals $10,000 or more. For senior associates and associates, purchases of $5,000 or more must be approved by their direct supervisors.__

After the direct supervisor has reviewed the request, they can either click “approve request” or “deny request” within the system. Once an option is selected, the direct supervisor will select “submit”, which will automatically generate a message to the purchase requestor to indicate whether the request has been approved or denied by the direct supervisor.

Only after approval has been obtained in the Portal should the requesting employee submit their desired purchase to the external vendor. Employees may not make purchases with the vendor before obtaining supervisor approval, nor will retroactive approvals be considered in compliance with this policy.

## Purchasing Controls Summary

| Control Objective / Attribute | Policy Requirement | System / Source Documentation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Supervisor Approval (Amount)** | • Senior associates and below: $\$5,000$ or more<br>• Manager and above: $\$10,000$ or more | Purchase Request Portal |
| **Supervisor Approval (Timing)** | Supervisor approval must be made before the purchase is submitted (externally) to the vendor. | Purchase Request Portal, Vendor Invoice |
| **Supervisor Approval (Provided by Appropriate Personnel)** | Only the direct supervisor of the employee seeking to make a purchase can provide authority to the purchase requestor.<br>*Example: To be compliant with this policy, a manager receives approval from their assigned director only.* | Purchase Request Portal, Internal Organization Chart |
| **Supervisor Approval (Automated Assignments)** | Each year, after the annual promotion cycle, HR provides an updated Internal Organization Chart. A member of the IT team compares the updated Org Chart to the Org Chart within the purchase request portal, and updates (within the system) for any differences/out-of-date information. | Purchase Request Portal, Internal Organization Chart |
